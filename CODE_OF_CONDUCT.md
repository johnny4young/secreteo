# Secreteo Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Secreteo is built for people with different languages, backgrounds, abilities, and levels of experience. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to make participation welcoming, respectful, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.
- Focus criticism on the work, never on the person.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's identity, recordings, dictated text, or private correspondence without clear permission.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when appropriate.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or knowingly spreading false information.
- Publishing private information or private material without consent.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This code applies in Secreteo repositories, issues, pull requests, community spaces, and when someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately through GitHub's [private reporting form](https://github.com/johnny4young/secreteo/security/advisories/new) and say that it is a conduct report. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details.

The maintainer will review reports fairly, preserve confidentiality as far as practical, and may remove content, close participation, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict a participant. Knowingly false or abusive reports may themselves violate this code. Retaliation is not tolerated.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/) and adapted to Secreteo's privacy-first community.
