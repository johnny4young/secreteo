# Product

Secreteo is global dictation for the Mac: local, private, and in the menu bar. It is MIT and free.

The name comes from Spanish *secretear*, to speak quietly to someone in confidence.

## What it does

- Dictate into any app with a shortcut, Fn hold, or mouse button.
- On-device recognition with Parakeet.
- Text modes, recipes, voice corrections, and capitalization that follows the caret.
- Polish and Rewrite through Apple Foundation Models or Ollama.
- Translate while pasting.
- Local history, stats and search, off until you turn them on.
- Shortcuts actions and a read-only MCP server.
- English and Spanish.

## What it does not do

- Meetings, which belong to [Portavoz](https://github.com/johnny4young/portavoz).
- Accounts, telemetry, or cloud speech recognition.
