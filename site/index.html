Secreteo
Español

Global, local, private dictation

Hold a key, speak, and the text lands in whatever app you are using. Speech recognition runs on your Mac.

Install

brew install --cask johnny4young/tap/secreteo
Download the latest release

Requirements

  • macOS 26 or later
  • Apple silicon, with 16 GB of memory recommended

Permissions

PermissionWhy
MicrophoneTo hear you while you dictate
AccessibilityTo detect the trigger key and place text in the active app

What leaves your Mac

Nothing by default. Audio and text stay on the device.

Network useWhen
Speech model download from Hugging FaceOnce per model, after you agree
Update checkOn schedule or when you ask
Polish through OllamaOnly if you set it up, to the address you choose
